The moment

Malawi’s digital opportunity

Malawi stands at an important moment in its digital transformation. The Internet creates opportunities for education, innovation and economic growth, yet challenges remain in accessibility, affordability, resilience and online safety.

19% of Malawi’s population used the Internet in 2024, according to Internet Society Pulse — highlighting the need for collective action.

Since its recognition in 2024 and official launch in July 2026, our Chapter has laid a foundation for an open, inclusive and secure Internet in Malawi. I recognise the efforts of the founding leadership and members who made this progress possible.

With a background in information technology, networking and systems development, I seek to contribute my skills, commitment and fresh perspective to this mission.

My vision

A Chapter that delivers

To support a sustainable, transparent, member-driven and digitally empowered Chapter that contributes to Malawi’s digital transformation and the global 2030 Strategy.

Five priorities

If entrusted as Secretary

I commit to these five priorities for our Chapter.

01

Strengthening Institutional Governance

I will promote accurate documentation, timely meeting records, structured action tracking and effective follow-up on resolutions, preserving institutional knowledge and ensuring continuity across leadership transitions.

02

Enhancing Communication and Member Engagement

I will advocate for regular member updates, a coordinated activity calendar and accessible feedback mechanisms, ensuring members have meaningful opportunities to remain informed, contribute ideas and participate in Chapter activities.

03

Digital Innovation and Knowledge Development

Building upon existing digital platforms, I will champion a complementary digital innovation portal to strengthen accountability, promote research and advance digital inclusion in Malawi. Drawing on my software development expertise, I propose three initiatives:

Chapter Impact Tracker

A data-driven platform to monitor activities, measure impact and identify geographical areas, communities and thematic priorities not yet reached by the Chapter — supporting evidence-based planning, resource mobilisation and informed decision-making.

Internet Research Repository

A centralised platform for documenting and sharing research, technical projects, publications and innovations undertaken by members, fostering collaboration, preserving institutional knowledge and encouraging research into Malawi’s Internet development challenges.

Chichewa Digital Safety Toolkit

An accessible, mobile-friendly resource providing practical cybersecurity education in Chichewa, empowering communities with knowledge of online fraud, digital privacy, cyber threats and responsible Internet use while addressing language barriers to digital literacy.

These initiatives will be pursued collaboratively with the Executive Council, members and stakeholders, subject to feasibility and approval. My commitment is to leverage technology to strengthen institutional efficiency and deliver measurable, inclusive and sustainable impact across Malawi.

04

Supporting Strategic Alignment and Accountability

I will work with the Executive Council to align Chapter activities with national Internet development priorities and the global 2030 Strategy through structured planning, documentation and periodic progress reporting.

05

Promoting Capacity Building and Member Opportunities

I will support timely dissemination of training, mentorship, fellowship, volunteering and funding opportunities, encouraging participation by young professionals, students, women and underserved communities in Internet development.

My commitment

Serve. Listen. Deliver.

Leadership is a responsibility to serve, listen and deliver. I commit to integrity, professionalism, accountability and collaborative leadership, guided by the Chapter’s bylaws and shared values.

Together, let us build upon the foundations already established and contribute to a Malawi where the Internet is not a privilege for a few, but a resource that empowers everyone.

Connect. Communicate. Deliver.
